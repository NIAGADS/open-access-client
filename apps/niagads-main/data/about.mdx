## Data Repository and Access

NIAGADS houses genomic, multi-omics, phenotypic, and related research data contributed by AD/ADRD
studies and research programs. Controlled-access datasets, which require a <a href="https://niagads.scrollhelp.site/support/application-instructions" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">data access request</a>,
include whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing, genotype data, functional genomic and other omics data, and associated phenotype
information.

Access is managed through the <a href="https://dss.niagads.org">NIAGADS Data Sharing Service</a> (DSS), with data shared according to NIH
policy and the data-use limitations established by the contributing studies and institutions.
Researchers can discover available datasets, submit Data Access Requests, and access approved data
through the DSS.

## Open Access Resources

NIAGADS also develops and maintains public resources that make AD/ADRD genomic data and knowledge
available without a Data Access Request. Major resources include:

- **NIAGADS GenomicsDB** <LinkOut href="https://www.niagads.org/genomics" label="Visit NIAGADS GenomicsDB" /> — integrates AD/ADRD GWAS summary statistics, ADSP sequence-derived
  variants and annotations, and other genomic evidence, with gene-, variant-, and region-based
  exploration.
- **FILER** <LinkOut href="https://filer2.niagads.org/" label="Visit FILER" /> — provides harmonized functional genomics and annotation datasets across human genome
  assemblies.
- **ADSP FunGen xQTL Browser** <LinkOut href="https://xqtl.niagads.org" label="Visit ADSP FunGen xQTL Browser" /> — provides access to AD/ADRD-relevant expression and other molecular
  QTL results.
- **NIAGADS Open Access Data** <LinkOut href="https://dss.niagads.org/open-access-data-portal/" label="Visit NIAGADS Open Access Data" /> — provides downloadable datasets that can be distributed without
  controlled-access authorization.
- **NIAGADS Open Access API** <LinkOut href="https://api.niagads.org" label="Visit NIAGADS Open Access API" /> — provides programmatic access to NIAGADS open-access resources,
  including GenomicsDB, FILER, and partner sites like ADVP.

Together, these resources support finding relevant datasets to examining
individual genes and variants, evaluating published genetic evidence, and incorporating NIAGADS data
into computational analyses.

## Alzheimer’s Disease Sequencing Project

NIAGADS has served as the Data Coordinating Center for the <a href="https://adseq.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Alzheimer’s Disease Sequencing Project
(ADSP)</a> since 2012. NIAGADS supports the organization, harmonization, distribution, and long-term
stewardship of ADSP genomic data and associated resources.

## Support for the Research Community

NIAGADS is happy to provide expertise and guidance to researchers working with AD/ADRD genomic data. This
includes assistance with data discovery and access, data-sharing plans, letters of support for grant
applications, identification of appropriate external repositories and resources, and technical
guidance for the analysis and interpretation of datasets housed at NIAGADS. If you have questions for our team, please email <a href="mailto:help@niagads.org">help@niagads.org</a>.
