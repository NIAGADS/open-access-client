
## Introduction

The National Institute on Aging Genetics of Alzheimer's Disease Data Storage Site (NIAGADS) is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit the NIAGADS website, how that information is used, and the measures taken to protect it.

This policy applies to the NIAGADS main website at [www.niagads.org](https://www.niagads.org). Other NIAGADS services, particularly those requiring user registration or access to controlled research data, may have additional privacy and data-use requirements.

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the NIAGADS website, our servers and service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- Your public-facing IP address.
- Browser type and operating system.
- Date and time of your visit.
- Pages visited and resources accessed.
- Referring website addresses, when available.
- General information about how visitors interact with the website.

This information helps us maintain website functionality, monitor security, troubleshoot technical issues, and understand how our resources are used.

Although this information does not necessarily identify you by name, certain details, such as IP addresses and online identifiers, may be considered personal information under applicable privacy laws.

### Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide personal information when contacting NIAGADS, submitting a support request, or subscribing to email updates.

Depending on the service, this information may include your name, email address, institutional affiliation, and any additional information you choose to share.

We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide requested assistance, and deliver communications you have chosen to receive.

If you subscribe to NIAGADS email updates, you may unsubscribe using the instructions included in our emails or by contacting us.

The main NIAGADS website does not require users to create an account to access its publicly available content. Registration and authentication for other NIAGADS services are managed separately.

## How We Use Information

NIAGADS uses information collected through its website to:

- Operate, maintain, and improve website functionality.
- Understand how visitors access and use our research resources.
- Monitor website performance and identify technical problems.
- Maintain the security and integrity of our systems.
- Respond to questions, support requests, and other communications.
- Provide updates and announcements to individuals who have requested them.
- Fulfill applicable legal, institutional, and regulatory obligations.

Information is used for purposes related to the operation and improvement of NIAGADS services and support for the research community.

NIAGADS does not sell visitors' personal information.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device to support functionality, maintain preferences, or collect information about website usage.

The NIAGADS website and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

- Supporting website functionality and navigation.
- Remembering user preferences, where applicable.
- Measuring website traffic and usage patterns.
- Identifying and resolving technical issues.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some website features or related services.

## Website Analytics

NIAGADS may use website analytics services, including Google Analytics, to understand how visitors interact with our website.

Where enabled, these services may collect information such as pages visited, session duration, approximate geographic location, browser characteristics, and general usage patterns.

Analytics information helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our website, identify frequently used resources, and make improvements to navigation and content.

Google's collection and processing of information are governed by its own privacy policies. For additional information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How We Share Information

NIAGADS may share limited information with authorized service providers that support the operation, maintenance, security, and communication functions of our website.

Such providers may include website hosting services, analytics providers, and email communication platforms.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or institutional policy, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of NIAGADS services.

NIAGADS does not share personal information with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.

## Third-Party Websites and Services

The NIAGADS website contains links to external websites and research resources, including government agencies, academic institutions, scientific databases, and collaborating projects.

These external websites operate independently and may follow different privacy policies and data collection practices.

NIAGADS is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of external websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party services they access.

Some NIAGADS resources may also have separate authentication, data-access, or privacy requirements.

## Data Retention and Security

NIAGADS takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information collected through its website against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Access to personal information is limited according to operational needs and applicable institutional requirements.

Information is retained according to its purpose, applicable legal and institutional obligations, and relevant records management practices.

Although we take steps to protect information, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## International Visitors

The NIAGADS website is operated in the United States.

If you access the website from outside the United States, information collected through your use of the website may be processed or stored in the United States or other locations where our authorized service providers operate.

Applicable privacy laws and data protection standards may differ from those in your country of residence.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to:

- Request information about personal data collected or maintained about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
- Request deletion of certain personal information.
- Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities.
- Withdraw consent for processing activities that rely on consent, where applicable.

These rights may be subject to legal, institutional, and operational limitations.

If you have questions about your personal information or would like to submit a privacy-related request, please contact NIAGADS using the information below.

## Children's Privacy

The NIAGADS website is intended primarily for researchers, scientific professionals, and other individuals interested in Alzheimer's disease and related dementia research.

The website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and NIAGADS does not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the main website.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

NIAGADS may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in website functionality, data collection practices, applicable requirements, or institutional policies.

Any updates will be published on this page, along with a revised effective or last-updated date.

We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
