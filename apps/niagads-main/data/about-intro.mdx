# About NIAGADS

NIAGADS is an NIH designated controlled-access data repository for human genomic and
associated omics data related to Alzheimer’s Disease (AD) and Alzheimer’s Disease
and Related Dementias (ADRD). Our mission is to help our user base to identify datasets,
access stored data, and share resulting data with the research community to advance the understanding of AD/ADRD.

NIAGADS facilitates secure and compliant data sharing with researchers all over the world in alignment with NIH policies for Controlled Access Data Repositories (CADR).
