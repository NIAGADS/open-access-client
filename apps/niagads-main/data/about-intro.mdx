# About NIAGADS

NIAGADS is an NIH designated controlled-access data repository for human genomic and
associated omics data related to Alzheimer’s Disease (AD) and Alzheimer’s Disease
and Related Dementias (ADRD).

Over the last 10+ years, NIAGADS has developed its own data access request process in alignment
with NIH policies, allowing for secure and compliant data sharing with researchers all over the world.
